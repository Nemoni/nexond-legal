# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 14, 2026

**Last updated:** October 20, 2026

Nexond LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company, operates CDL Permit Test: Cited Prep (“CDL Cited”). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app, contact us, or open its linked pages. Contact us at [support@nexond.com](mailto:support@nexond.com) with privacy questions or requests.

CDL Cited is available for iPhone through the App Store and for Android phones through Google Play. “Your device” below means the iPhone or Android phone where you use the app.

## Information on your device

CDL Cited does not require an account. The app stores your selected state, study goal and tests, answers and practice history, missed questions, practice exam results and unfinished exams, question bank version, and a local record of your free trial or purchase status, including when a trial started, on your device. Nexond does not receive this study data automatically. You can keep using study features offline. Deleting the app removes its local data from that device; Nexond cannot restore it for you.

Your device's own backup may copy this data. On iPhone, Apple may handle device backups under your Apple settings and Apple's policies. On Android, the app allows Android backup of its study database (including your trial start time), so Google may store a copy in your Google account's backup and restore it when you reinstall the app or set up a new phone, depending on your device's backup settings and [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Nexond cannot access these backups.

## Purchases and free trial

The 3-day (72-hour) free trial does not turn into a paid subscription and does not charge you when it ends. Nexond does not receive your payment card details.

- **App Store (iPhone):** If you start the free trial, buy Unlock forever, or restore a purchase, Apple handles the transaction through the App Store. The app reads purchase and trial status from Apple and keeps a local status record so it can unlock the right features. See [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) for Apple's handling of App Store information.
- **Google Play (Android):** The free trial is recorded only on your phone: when you tap Start 3-day free trial, the app saves the start time on the device, with no Google Play transaction. If you buy Unlock forever or restore a purchase, Google handles the transaction through Google Play. The app asks Google Play for your purchases when it starts and when you restore, and keeps a local status record so it can unlock the right features. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for Google's handling of Google Play information.

## Question bank updates and web pages

When online, the app checks for a new question bank and may download an encrypted bank file from `data.nexond.com`, hosted by Cloudflare. These requests do not include your name, email address, answers, study history, or an app-specific user identifier. Nexond does not keep individual request logs. Cloudflare may process technical request information, including your IP address, to deliver and protect the service under [its privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

When you open a state manual, the app downloads the official manual PDF directly from the state agency's website and keeps a copy on your device so it can show the cited page. That website receives your IP address and other technical request information under its own policies.

Our legal pages at `legal.nexond.com` are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security and handles other web request information under [its privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). The app may also open official manuals, laws, or other external sites in an in-app browser. Those sites may receive your IP address and use information under their own privacy policies. Nexond does not control their practices.

## Emails you choose to send

The app never sends an email without your action. If you use Report a problem, Contact us, or Request my state, it opens your email app with a draft to `support@nexond.com`. A question report includes the question ID, selected state, app version, and question bank version. A feedback draft includes the state and both versions. A state request has a blank field for you to fill in. You can edit or discard any draft.

If you send a message, we receive your sender address, the message, and any information you include. We use it to reply, investigate a reported issue, or consider a requested state. Your email provider and ours process the message to deliver it. We keep support messages only as long as reasonably needed for those purposes or as required by law. You can ask us to delete a message at the address above; we will honor the request where the law allows.

## Other disclosures and your choices

CDL Cited has no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDK. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not use the app to track you across other companies' apps or websites. The app may ask Apple or Google to show a rating prompt; Apple or Google handles any rating or review you choose to submit.

We do not use browser Do Not Track signals to change the app's behavior because we do not conduct cross-site tracking. We do not allow third-party trackers in the app to collect information about your activities over time across unrelated apps or websites. Hosting and external sites may apply their own policies to those signals. You can stop update checks by using the app offline, choose not to send an email, or delete the app to remove its local study data. The only personal information you choose to provide directly to Nexond is in an email; hosting providers also process the technical request data described above. There is no in-app consent-based collection to turn off. You can withdraw the choice to email us by not sending a draft or by asking us to delete a message you sent, subject to applicable law. Depending on your state, you may have rights to know, access, correct, or delete personal information held by us and to exercise those rights without discrimination. Email us to make a request; we will respond as applicable law requires. We cannot retrieve or delete study data that remains only on your device.

## Security

The app downloads the question bank and opens web pages over HTTPS, so they are encrypted in transit. Study data stays in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read; protect it with your device's screen lock. Support email is kept in a password-protected mailbox that only Nexond can access, and we delete messages as described above. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we limit what we hold to the emails you choose to send.

## Children

CDL Cited is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If we learn that a child under 13 sent us personal information, we will delete it unless the law requires otherwise. A parent or guardian can contact us at the address above.

## Changes

We will post changes to this policy at this URL and update the Last updated date. For a material change, we will also state its new Effective date and give any additional notice required by law before it takes effect. Please review this page when you use the app.
