# CDL Cited Sources

**Last updated:** November 1, 2026

CDL Permit Test: Cited Prep (“CDL Cited”) is an unofficial study app made by Nexond LLC. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting for FMCSA, the U.S. Department of Transportation, any state DMV or DPS, or AAMVA.

Every question is original and written from the official government publications below. Each answer in the app cites its source; tap the citation to open the rule or the manual page it comes from. Real test questions come from official, non-public question pools, and no app can show them.

## Federal rules

- Federal Motor Carrier Safety Regulations and Hazardous Materials Regulations, 49 CFR: <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49>

## State laws

- Texas Transportation Code (Texas Commercial Rules test): <https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/>
