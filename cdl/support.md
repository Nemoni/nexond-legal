# CDL Cited Support

CDL Permit Test: Cited Prep (“CDL Cited”) is an unofficial CDL permit study app for iPhone and Android phones, made by Nexond LLC. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to FMCSA, the U.S. Department of Transportation, any state DMV or DPS, or AAMVA.

## Contact us

Email [support@nexond.com](mailto:support@nexond.com). In the app, Settings › Contact us opens a draft with your state, app version, and question bank version filled in.

## Report a problem with a question

Tap the flag (Report a problem) in the top-right corner of the question screen. Your email app opens a draft that already includes the question ID, your state, and the app and question bank versions. Tell us what looks wrong; a page or section from your state manual helps us check it quickly.

## Restore a purchase

Open Settings › Restore purchase, or tap Already purchased? Restore on the welcome screen. Unlock forever is a one-time purchase, and the free trial never turns into a subscription.

- **iPhone:** Use the same Apple ID you bought with. Refunds are handled by Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/).
- **Android:** Sign in to Google Play with the same Google account you bought with. Refunds are handled by Google; see [Google Play refund policies](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637). The Android free trial is kept on your phone, not in Google Play, so Restore purchase does not restore it. If you reinstall, Android may bring back your progress and trial start time from your Google backup.

## Request your state

If your state is not listed yet, tap Request my state on the state screen, or email us the state name.
